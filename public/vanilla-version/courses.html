ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

Secondary School Curriculum & Subject Clusters

St. Mary's Secondary School delivers the 4-tier secondary education syllabus (Form 1 through Form 4).

LEVEL 1

Form 1 (Freshman)

Foundation year covering all introductory subjects and laboratory safety training.

LEVEL 2

Form 2 (Sophomore)

Intermediate theory, algebraic fundamentals, and career guidance seminars.

LEVEL 3

Form 3 (Junior)

Elective specialization: students select core 8-9 examination subjects.

LEVEL 4

Form 4 (Candidate)

National examination revision clinics, university preparation, and leadership capstones.
